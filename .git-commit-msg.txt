diagrams: three registers + blueprint cross-sections

A redraw wave on the six cross-section diagrams, the spec change
behind it, and the documentation around it.

Why a redraw
------------

The 2026-05-22 wave landed the diagram pipeline (optional
`diagrams:` schema field, `attachDiagramSvg` loader, `<figure>`
rendering on the concept detail page) and a full set of 10
deterministic SVGs. The six hardware schematics in that set
(mash-tun, washback, worm-tub, shell-and-tube-condenser,
coffey-still, spirit-safe) used the project's sketch register —
the `feTurbulence` + `feDisplacementMap` wobble filter at scale 6.

Reviewed against the user's eye, the sketch register breathes
fine on the pot-still (parts spatially distinct, margin around
each element) but actively hurts cross-section views. Interior
detail — perforated-plate slot ticks, tube bundles, worm coils,
switcher arms, test cups, mullions — smears into containing walls
under the displacement filter. The wobble is competing with the
strokes it's trying to draw.

Spec change
-----------

`docs/diagram-style.md` extended from two visual registers to
three:

- **Sketch** (turbulence + displacement filter) — pot still,
  production-chain flowchart, peating-measurement matrix. The
  external-assembly view.
- **Strict** (no filter, precise axes, full grid, plotted from a
  sourced figure) — spirit-cut, cask-maturation-kinetics. The
  data-graph view.
- **Technical-schematic** (clean lines, no filter) — the six
  cross-sections. New register.

The honest-about-indicativeness argument that grounded the sketch
register is preserved but reframed: it's the *absence of
dimensions* that signals "indicative, not metrological", and both
the sketch register (via wobble) and the technical-schematic
register (via simple absence) honour that.

Blueprint conventions
---------------------

Within the technical-schematic register, the spec also documents
the conventions settled in the 2026-05-26 pilot on mash-tun:

- `stroke="currentColor"` throughout. Some SVG viewers do not
  resolve `var(--muted-color)` when applied as `stroke` (only as
  `fill`), so var()-stroked leaders render invisibly in those
  viewers — diagnosed live in pilot. `currentColor` resolves
  reliably. Fills may still use `var()`.
- Double-walled vessel outlines (outer + inner, 4 user-units
  apart) with generic diagonal section hatching between, via SVG
  `<pattern>` + `fill-rule="evenodd"`. Generic hatching only —
  ANSI/ISO material-specific hatching remains forbidden.
- Dashed annotation leaders (`stroke-dasharray="5 3"`,
  stroke-width 1.0) ending in filled-circle dots
  (`fill="currentColor"`, r=2.4 for annotation, r=2.6 for nozzle
  endpoints).
- Short solid three-sided pipe-nozzle stubs at every inlet and
  outlet so the dashed annotation leader points at a visible
  structural part, not at empty interior space.
- Dashed hidden lines (`stroke-dasharray="3 2"`) for occluded
  interior parts — e.g., the rake teeth on the far side of the
  rotating arm.
- Explicit stroke-weight hierarchy (1.4 outlines, 1.2 internal
  primary, 1.0 secondary and leaders, 0.7-0.8 fine detail, 0.5
  hatch pattern).
- `stroke-linecap="butt"` on lines with arrowheads or dashed
  endings (avoids the round-cap half-circle artifact).
- No flow-direction arrowheads. The label text carries direction.
  Mixing flow arrows on pipes with dot terminations on
  annotation leaders produced unreadable double-arrow
  appearances during the pilot.
