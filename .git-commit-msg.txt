concepts: wrapper pages + the educational-page diagram set

Two waves of work that had accumulated uncommitted on the working
tree, committed together.

Wave 1 — concept wrapper layer
------------------------------

Closes the wrapper-layer gap in the concept pages. The
production-chain coverage build had completed the detail pages
and given distillation and maturation cluster wrappers, but there
was no top-level overview tying the whole chain together, and
malting / mashing / fermentation had no stage wrapper. This wave
adds the master overview and the three missing stage wrappers:

- educational/whisky-production — the master "How Scotch whisky
  is made" overview. Sequences the entire chain (raw materials,
  malting, milling, mashing, fermentation, distillation,
  maturation, blending/bottling, classification) and links every
  stage. The natural encyclopedia entry point.
- educational/malting, educational/mashing,
  educational/fermentation — stage wrappers matching the existing
  distillation and maturation wrappers; each a short overview
  linking that stage's detail pages.

All four at confidence: high, grounded on Russell 3rd ed. and
Miller 2024. Densely cross-linked per the brief — every
referenceable concept linked inline, not just in
related_concepts. The distillation / maturation / blending
wrappers were given back-links up to the new master so the
hierarchy is navigable both ways. A stale "(queued)" reference to
the Coffey-still page was fixed in educational/distillation — that
page exists (Tier 2) and is now linked. Concept-page total
81 -> 85.

Wave 2 — educational-page diagram set
-------------------------------------

Authors the diagram set for the educational and equipment concept
pages, against the pipeline proven by the pot-still pilot.

- Schema: the concept schema gains an optional `diagrams:` field
  (a list of {file, alt, caption?, source_id?}). Additive within
  concept.template.yml v0.1; existing entries stay valid.
  CHANGELOG [0.8.2].
- Rendering: site/src/lib/data.ts inlines each diagram's SVG at
  build time (attachDiagramSvg); the concept detail page renders
  a <figure> with caption and source citation.
- Ten SVGs in data/diagrams/, attached to their concept entries.
- Authoring spec: docs/diagram-style.md, which codifies two
  visual registers. Hardware and process schematics use a
  hand-drawn sketch register — a pure-SVG feTurbulence +
  feDisplacementMap wobble filter, no JavaScript, fixed seed for
  determinism. Data graphs use a strict register — no filter,
  precise axes, full grid, plotted exactly from a sourced figure.
- The two strict data graphs — spirit-cut (DMTS through the
  spirit-still run, after Miller Fig. 6.7) and
  cask-maturation-kinetics (solids extraction vs cask age, after
  Miller Fig. 8.11) — are emitted by the new committed generator
  scripts/gen_data_diagrams.py, from data points digitised off
  the cited figures. cask-maturation-kinetics gains a new
  source 6 (Miller, Whisky Science 2nd ed., Ch 8, Fig. 8.11).

Settled in this wave: pot-still, the production-chain flowchart,
the peating-measurement matrix, both strict graphs. The six
hardware schematics (mash-tun, washback, worm-tub,
shell-and-tube-condenser, coffey-still, spirit-safe) are committed
as first-draft checkpoints — a redraw is queued in TODO.md
Beta-readiness against specific clarity feedback.
