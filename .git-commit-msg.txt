Literature scouting
-------------------

New doc docs/literature-scouting.md — the scouting / shopping
list of reference works worth acquiring plus open-access sources
citable now (the complement to bibliography.md, which catalogues
works already in use). A web-search pass surfaced the MDPI
journal Beverages (fully open access) and the Journal of the
Institute of Brewing (open access from 2023) as usable without
acquisition, and Miller 2024 as the highest-value acquisition
for the confidence blocker.

Three works registered as held (all user-supplied):

- Miller, Whisky Science: A Condensed Distillation, 2nd ed.
  (2024, Springer, ISBN 978-3-031-50686-4) — genuinely
  independent of the Russell lineage; the task-2 unblock.
- Russell/Stewart/Kellershohn, Whisky and Other Spirits, 3rd ed.
  (2022) — currency upgrade only; NOT an independent source (its
  chapters covering the affected entries share authors with the
  2nd edition — Nicol, Russell & Stewart, Bringhurst, Conner).
- la Grange-Nel, Characterisation and Improvement of Whiskey
  Yeast (2003 MSc thesis) — supplementary tier; useful for its
  Chapter 2 literature review and as a bibliography-mining route.

Miller grounding pass
---------------------

Miller 2024 grounded against the seven medium-confidence glossary
entries. Five promoted medium -> high, each now citing Russell +
Miller with page-level source notes:

- kiln          — Miller Ch 3 Malting, pp 151-152
- mashing        — Miller Ch 4 Mashing, pp 187-199
- fermentation  — Miller Ch 5 Fermentation, pp 219-241
- reflux        — Miller Ch 6, pp 253-255
- wash-still    — Miller Ch 6-7, Distillation

Two held at medium:

- distillers-yeast — Miller corroborates the distilling-strain
  core (Distillers Company "M" strain; high attenuation;
  distilling-vs-brewing distinction) but not the entry's named
  yeast-house list, which still needs a producer / SWRI
  cross-check. Miller added as a source; confidence unchanged.
- shell-and-tube — Miller does not treat condenser types
  anywhere in the book; no second source available, entry
  untouched.

One source conflict surfaced and preserved per the
source-conflict policy, not smoothed: Miller gives the first
mashing water at ca. 70 C; Russell gives a first-water strike of
63.5-64 C.
