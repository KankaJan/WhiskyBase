Glenfiddich
-----------

- Location: Dufftown, Banffshire, Speyside.
- Owner: William Grant & Sons Ltd — unbroken family ownership
  since the 1886 founding. One of the few sizeable Scotch
  producers still entirely family-owned; a structural parallel
  to the unrelated Grant family's Glenfarclas.
- Founded 1886 (build start); first spirit Christmas Day 1887.
  The producer's own timeline is internally inconsistent on the
  1886/1887 build-start year — recorded as founded 1886,
  first_spirit 1887, with the conflict noted in the entry. The
  original stills were second-hand equipment bought from the
  Cardhu distillery.
- The distillery most associated with the creation of the
  modern marketed single-malt category — the 1963 decision by
  Sandy Grant Gordon to actively market single malt outside
  Scotland.
- Very large scale: ~21 million LPA; 48 Douglas-fir washbacks
  (~74-hour average fermentation); 43 copper stills (16 wash,
  27 spirit), with 15 of the 27 spirit stills direct-fired by
  gas. On-site coppersmiths (since 1957) and cooperage (since
  1959). Over one million casks in warehouse.
- Bottlings: 12 Year Old (40% ABV, bourbon + oloroso flagship)
  and 15 Year Old Solera (40% ABV) — the latter is the first
  populated bottling exercising a solera-style continuous-
  marrying vatting stage (the 1998 Solera Vat, never drained
  below half-full, inspired by the Spanish sherry solera).

Critical-eval fix: the "world's largest-selling single malt"
claim was softened to "among the world's best-selling single
malts", with an explicit note that Glenfiddich and Glenlivet
trade the top-selling position depending on market and measure.

Site — /production-lines/ and /bottlings/ indexes regrouped
------------------------------------------------------------

Both index pages rewritten from a single flat table to a grouped
list: entries are now listed under their parent distillery as h2
sub-headings, distilleries in alphabetical order, each heading
linking to the distillery page.

- production-lines: grouped on the line's `distillery` field.
  Multi-line distilleries (Springbank x3, Bunnahabhain x2) carry
  a "N lines" count on the heading.
- bottlings: grouped on `produced_at_distillery`. The redundant
  per-row Distillery column is dropped (it is now the group
  heading); distilleries with more than one release carry a
  "N releases" count. The OB/IB type column is retained.

Rationale: a flat exhaustive table does not scale as the data
grows — the bottlings table had already reached 39 rows. A
dropdown was rejected — JS-dependent, hides content behind a
control, invisible to Pagefind body-indexing, and cuts against
the site's Wikipedia content-first design. An alphabet jump-bar
was rejected as overkill. The grouped-list pattern matches
casks/index (grouped by category) and the concept sub-indexes —
plain content, no JS, every line a real link.

Both tables use `table-layout: fixed` with explicit widths on the
narrow columns (Peating/Confidence; ABV/Age/Type) so column edges
align across every distillery group rather than each table sizing
its columns independently. astro check clean (only the
pre-existing @types/node errors; no new warnings from either
rewrite).
