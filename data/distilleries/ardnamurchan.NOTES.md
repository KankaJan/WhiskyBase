22nd distillery; second of the 2026-06-14 new-wave batch. Confidence medium
(Wikipedia + specialist-retailer references).

- **Independent-bottler-owned.** Built and owned by Adelphi, an independent
  bottler — like Ardnahoe (Hunter Laing). A distinct ownership pattern from
  the majors; recorded as `current: Adelphi Distillery Ltd`, `parent: null`.
- **Peated + unpeated on one line.** The distillery alternates peated and
  unpeated campaigns on the same stills, and the core malt vats them ~50:50.
  Modelled as a single production line with `peating.measurements: []`,
  `peat_origin: null`, and a note — rather than two lines or a forced ppm —
  since no single phenol spec applies and the peated-campaign ppm is not
  firmly sourced.

  composition; a practice with no concept page yet (candidate:
  `practice/cask-composition-transparency`, alongside Bruichladdich's
